FAQ

Questions people ask before paying.

Something else on your mind? Ask me on WhatsApp.

How much do domain and hosting cost, and who pays?

You do, directly, and everything is registered in your name. A domain is a yearly fee. Hosting for most small business sites costs little or nothing. I'll tell you the exact amounts before we start, so there are no surprise renewals.

Do I really own the website?

Yes. At launch you get the full source code in your own GitHub account, plus every login: domain, hosting, database, payment dashboard and admin panel. You can hire anyone to work on it after me.

What if you disappear after I pay?

You only pay 50% to start, and the rest after you've seen and approved the finished build. You get preview links every week, so you always see real progress. And because everything is in your name, nothing is ever held hostage.

How long will it take?

Websites: 5–7 days. Website + Backend: 2–3 weeks. Apps: 4–6 weeks. The exact date goes in your written plan. The biggest cause of delay is waiting on content, so having your text and photos ready helps.

Can I update the website myself?

On the ₦300k and ₦500k plans, yes — you get an admin dashboard to change products, prices, menus and content without touching code. On the ₦100k plan, small text and photo changes are covered by the care plan, or quoted per change.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo (if you have one), photos of your products or work, and the basic information about your business. Not sure what to write? I'll help shape the words.

Is ₦500,000 really enough for an app?

For a focused first version, yes. Before I start, we write down exactly which features are included. Anything outside that list is quoted separately, so the price you approved never changes on you.

Can I pay in instalments?

Every project is split 50% at the start and 50% at launch. For ₦500k projects we can split it into three milestones instead. You always get an invoice and a receipt.

Do you work with businesses outside Nigeria?

Yes — Mama Gee is a restaurant in Canada. Everything happens over WhatsApp and video calls, wherever you are.

Tell me what your business needs.

I'll reply with a plan and a fixed price. No pressure, no jargon.
